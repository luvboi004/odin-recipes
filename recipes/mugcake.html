Chocolate Mug Cake

Description

A quick single-serving chocolate cake mixed and cooked in a microwave-safe mug. It is handy when you want a small dessert without baking a whole cake.

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cocoa powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
  • 3 tablespoons milk
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil or melted butter
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)
  • Chocolate chips (optional)

Steps

  1. Use a large microwave-safe mug. Add the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, and baking powder, then mix.
  2. Add the milk, oil or melted butter, and vanilla. Stir until no dry patches remain.
  3. Fold in a few chocolate chips if you like.
  4. Microwave for about 60 to 90 seconds. Cooking time varies by microwave, so check that the centre is set.
  5. Let the mug cool briefly before eating.
