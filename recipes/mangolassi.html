Mango Lassi

Description

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped ripe mango (or mango pulp)
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt (curd)
  • 1/4 cup chilled milk (optional for consistency)
  • 2-3 tsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • A pinch of cardamom powder (optional)
  • Ice cubes (optional)
  1. Add chopped mango, yogurt, sugar, and cardamom powder into a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth. Add milk to adjust the consistency if too thick.
  3. Add a few ice cubes and blend again for a few seconds.
  4. Pour into a glass and serve chilled. Garnish with chopped pistachios or mint if desired.
