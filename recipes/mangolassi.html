Mango Lassi

Description

Mango lassi is a chilled yogurt drink blended with ripe mango. A little cardamom adds a fragrant flavour, making it a refreshing drink for warm days.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped ripe mango or mango pulp
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt (curd)
  • 1/4 cup chilled milk, as needed
  • 2 to 3 teaspoons sugar, adjusted to taste
  • A pinch of cardamom powder (optional)
  • Ice cubes (optional)

Steps

  1. Add the mango, yogurt, sugar, and cardamom powder to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth. Add a little milk if the lassi is too thick.
  3. Add ice cubes and blend briefly, if desired.
  4. Pour into a glass and serve chilled.
