Creamy Garlic Alfredo Pasta

Description

A creamy garlic pasta made with a simple butter, garlic, and cream sauce. It is a comforting meal that is easy to prepare at home.

Ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup cooking cream
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water according to the package instructions. Reserve a little pasta water, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium-low heat and cook the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the cream and Parmesan. Stir gently until the sauce is smooth.
  4. Add the cooked pasta and toss to coat. Loosen the sauce with a little reserved pasta water if needed.
  5. Season with salt and black pepper, then serve warm.
