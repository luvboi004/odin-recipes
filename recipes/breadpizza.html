Bread Pizza

Description

Bread pizza is a quick snack made with bread slices, pizza sauce, vegetables, and melted cheese. It can be prepared in a covered pan on the stovetop.

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons pizza sauce
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small capsicum, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup grated mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Oregano, chilli flakes, and salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Spread a thin layer of pizza sauce over each bread slice.
  2. Add the chopped onion and capsicum, then top with grated cheese.
  3. Sprinkle oregano, chilli flakes, and a little salt.
  4. Heat butter in a pan over low heat. Place the bread slices in the pan and cover with a lid.
  5. Cook for about 5 to 7 minutes, or until the base is crisp and the cheese has melted. Serve warm.
